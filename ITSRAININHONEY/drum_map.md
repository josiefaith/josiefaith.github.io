## Velocity conventions

| Range | Meaning | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| 20–40 | Ghost notes | quiet snare taps, hi-hat foot, soft shaker 16ths |
| 40–65 | Soft / restrained | verse hats and cross-stick, half-time bridge, pull-back, outro |
| 65–95 | Normal playing | verse kick, chorus snare, hats and ride, fills |
| 95–120 | Accents | crashes, final-chorus backbeats, fill peaks |

Timing drifts by at most ±4 ticks. Downbeats are never early.

## The groove

The kick, the bass and the piano left hand share one rhythm in each section, so they lock:

- **Verses:** 1, the "a" of 2, the "and" of 3 (16ths 0, 7, 10). Cross-stick on 2 and 4 with the piano's
  backbeat chords. That syncopated kick is what makes it bounce.
- **Pre-choruses:** 1, the "and" of 2, 3, the "and" of 4. Real snare on 2 and 4.
- **Choruses:** 1, the "and" of 2, the "and" of 3 (the final chorus adds the "and" of 1).

## What the drums do in each section

- **Intro (1-4):** piano alone for two bars, then soft hats and a kick, cross-stick pickup.
- **Verse 1 (5-12):** syncopated kick, cross-stick on 2 and 4, 8th hats. Open hat at the end.
- **Verse 2 (13-20):** same groove plus ghost snares and a 16th "skip" in the hats.
- **Pre-Chorus 1 (21-24):** real snare, kick on the "ands"; snare pickup into the chorus.
- **Chorus 1 (25-32):** half the kit: kick on 1 and the "and" of 3, soft snare, soft hats. One crash.
- **Turnaround (33-34):** kick, cross-stick on 4, open hat.
- **Verse 3 (35-42):** the verse 2 groove; shaker joins.
- **Pre-Chorus 2 (43-46):** builds to a tom fill, high to low, landing on two snares.
- **Chorus 2 (47-54):** first full-band chorus: crashes, ghost notes, hats with open-hat lifts, shaker.
- **Bridge (55-62):** half-time (cross-stick, then snare, on beat 3), then climbs back into time
  and ends with growing snare 8ths.
- **Breakdown (63-64):** hat foot only under the piano hook, then four on the floor and a snare pickup.
- **Final Chorus (65-72):** biggest: ride with bell on the beats, extra kick, tambourine on 2 and 4.
  Stops on beat 3 of the last bar.
- **Pull-back Chorus (73-76):** kick, cross-stick on 4, hat foot; snare build in the last bar.
- **Last Lift (77-80):** full band again; tom fill into the outro.
- **Outro (81-86):** verse groove, then ride quarters, then one soft kick + crash on the last downbeat.
