Guitar: capo on fret 2, open shapes only, no barre chords, all within four frets of the capo. The shape name is what your fingers make with the capo on. × means don't play that string; ○ means play it open. Numbers in the dots are fretting fingers: 1 = index, 2 = middle, 3 = ring, 4 = pinky. Shapes marked "easier version" leave out one colour note, which the piano still plays.

Feel: bright and bouncy, straight 16ths. The kick, bass and piano left hand share a syncopated rhythm in the verses (1, the "a" of 2, the "and" of 3) while the piano answers on 2 and 4.

Room for your voice: the piano plays its hook only in the intro (1–4), turnaround (33–34), breakdown (63) and outro tag (81–85). Everywhere else it only comps.

Handwritten notes: what the band does: entrances, fills, stops. The tempo slows over bars 85–86.
